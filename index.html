Written in the third week

Dear Irdyna,

I wrote this letter a few times and deleted it every time. The things that matter most are the hardest to say well. So I will keep it simple and honest. That is how I want to be with you always.

It has only been three weeks. But in these three weeks you became the first person I think of when I wake up. You are also the last person I pray for before I sleep. I did not plan that. Alhamdulillah it just happened. It feels like a gift I do not want to waste.

Thank you for being gentle with me. You listen without making me feel small. You make it safe for me to be myself. Being near you makes me want to be a better man. I want to be more patient and more grateful and closer to Allah.

There is something I want to be honest about. At first I worried when I thought about other men around you. That was my own fear and never your fault. I am learning to let it go. You can have friends who are men just as I have one or two kawan perempuan. I trust you and I trust your good character. All I ask is that we stay honest with each other and keep our limits. I am still adapting. Please be patient with me and I will keep trying.

One more thing. Sometimes I may lose my mood for no reason. Please do not worry. Most of the time it is just because I am a guy HAHAHAHA and if I ever hear or see something about your past and go quiet please know it is not because of anything else. It does not change how I feel about you. I only need a little time and I will always come back to you.

I promise to respect you and protect your dignity. I promise to keep our limits because I want what we have to please Allah. I promise to speak with kindness and say sorry without excuses. I will never make you feel small. When the time is right insya-Allah I will come to you the proper way with our families.

I do not know what Allah has written for us. But every day I ask Him to protect us from every darkness and every sin. I ask Him to guide us to what is best and make this a source of peace for us both. Whatever He decides I want to stay a good man to you.

Ya Tuhan kami kurniakanlah kami penyejuk mata dan penenang hati pada insan yang kami sayangi dan jadikanlah kami pemimpin bagi orang yang bertakwa.

Thank you Irdyna for your patience and your laughter. An ordinary day feels softer with you in it. If you are tired then rest. If you are sad I am here. I want to walk toward the future with you with Allah at the center of us.

I love you Irdyna and I really miss you so much.

With all my heart,

Yours, always
